# Account and Login

## Reset a forgotten password
1. Go to the login page and select Forgot password.
2. Enter the email address registered on your account.
3. Open the reset link in the email within 30 minutes. Check your spam folder if the email has not arrived.
4. Choose a new password with at least 8 characters, including one number.

Reset emails can be requested up to 3 times per hour.

## Account locked
After 5 failed login attempts, your account is locked for 15 minutes. You can wait for the lock to expire, or reset your password to unlock the account immediately.

## Change your email or phone number
Go to Account Settings, then Profile, and update the details. Confirm the change with the code sent to the new address.

## Two-factor authentication
Turn it on under Account Settings, then Security. Store the backup codes somewhere safe, because they are the only way back in if you lose your phone.
