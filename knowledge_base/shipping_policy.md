# Shipping Policy

## Delivery times
Standard delivery takes 4 to 6 business days after dispatch. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days. Orders are dispatched within 1 business day of payment. Orders placed after 2 PM are dispatched the next business day.

## Tracking
Every order receives a tracking link by email within 24 hours of dispatch. Tracking updates can take up to 12 hours to appear on the carrier website.

## Late or missing orders
If your order has not arrived 2 business days after its estimated delivery date, contact support with your order ID. We open a carrier trace within 1 business day. If the carrier confirms the parcel is lost, or it has not arrived 10 business days after dispatch, we send a replacement or refund the full order value, whichever you choose.

## Address changes
Delivery addresses can be changed only before the order is dispatched. Once an order has shipped, the address cannot be changed.

## Shipping charges
Standard delivery is free on orders of 499 or more. Orders below that amount pay a flat shipping fee of 49. Express delivery always costs 99.
