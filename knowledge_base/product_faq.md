# Product FAQ

## Warranty
All kitchen appliances, including blenders, come with a 12-month manufacturer warranty against manufacturing faults. Warranty claims for faulty appliances follow the damaged item process in the refund policy.

## Appliance care
Rinse the blender jar straight after use and never run it dry. Do not immerse the motor base in water.

## Footwear sizing
Our shoes run half a size small for wide feet. Check the size chart on each product page before ordering.

## Stock and restocking
Out-of-stock items show an expected restock date on the product page. Select Notify me to receive an email when the item is available again.
