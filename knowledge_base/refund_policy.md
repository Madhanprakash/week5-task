# Refund and Return Policy

## Return window
You can return unused items within 30 days of the delivery date, in their original packaging. Items marked Final Sale cannot be returned.

## How to request a return
Open Orders in your account, choose the item and select Request return. Print the prepaid return label and drop the parcel at any partner courier point within 7 days of requesting it.

## Refund timing
We refund the original payment method within 5 business days after the returned item is scanned at our warehouse. Banks may take a further 3 to 5 business days to show the credit.

## Damaged, broken or defective items
Report a damaged, broken or defective item within 7 days of delivery, with photos of the item and its packaging. We offer a free replacement or a full refund. For a replacement, the new item is dispatched within 3 business days after the faulty item is scanned at our warehouse. If the replacement is out of stock, you receive a full refund instead.

## Exchanges
Size exchanges on clothing and footwear are possible within 30 days, subject to stock availability.

## Billing errors
If you were charged more than once for the same order, or charged for an order you did not place, contact support with your order ID. We investigate within 2 business days and refund any duplicate charge to the original payment method. Billing disputes that remain unresolved after 5 business days are reviewed by a senior support specialist.
