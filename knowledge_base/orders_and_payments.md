# Orders and Payments

## Cancelling an order
You can cancel an order at any time before it is dispatched. Open Orders in your account, choose the order and select Cancel order. Once an order has been dispatched it cannot be cancelled, but you can return it after delivery under the return policy. Refunds for cancelled orders go to the original payment method within 5 business days.

## Changing an order
Items, sizes and colours in an order cannot be edited after it is placed. To change an order, cancel it before dispatch and place a new one.

## Wrong or missing item
If you received the wrong item, or an item is missing from your order, report it within 7 days of delivery with your order ID and a photo of what you received and the packaging label. We send the correct item free of charge or give a full refund, whichever you choose.

## Payment failed but money deducted
If your payment failed or no order was created but the amount left your account, contact support with the payment date and amount. We check the payment within 2 business days. Any amount taken without a confirmed order is returned to the original payment method.

## Discount codes
Only one discount code can be used per order, and it must be entered before payment. Codes cannot be added after an order is placed. Each code has its own expiry date and minimum order value, shown in the offer.

## Failed delivery attempt
If the courier could not deliver your order, they try again on the next business day and make up to 3 attempts. After the last attempt the parcel returns to our warehouse and we refund the order value to the original payment method within 5 business days of receiving it.
